1. Information we collect

We may collect information you choose to provide when you contact us, submit an enquiry, request a trial, sign up for updates, or place an order. This can include your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, order details, and the information you include in your message.

Some technical information, such as browser type, device information, and pages visited, may also be collected when you use the website.

2. How we use information

We use submitted information to:

  • Respond to questions, contact forms, and support requests.
  • Process orders and provide delivery or order updates.
  • Arrange trial deliveries, pickups, and returns.
  • Maintain and improve the website and customer experience.
  • Meet applicable legal and record-keeping requirements.

3. Cookies and analytics

The website may use essential browser storage or cookies to remember preferences, such as your selected theme or text direction. If analytics tools are enabled, they may collect aggregated information about how visitors use the site.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling some cookies or storage may affect site features.

4. Sharing information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share relevant information with service providers who help operate the website, fulfil orders, process payments, or deliver communications. We require those providers to handle information appropriately.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when necessary to protect our rights, customers, or services.

5. Data security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. No online service can guarantee complete security, so please avoid sending sensitive payment details through general contact forms.

We keep information only for as long as needed to respond to your request, provide services, maintain records, or meet legal obligations.

6. Your choices

You can contact us to ask about, correct, or request the deletion of information you have provided, subject to applicable requirements. You may also unsubscribe from non-essential marketing messages using the instructions in those messages.
